One problem, three symptoms

Reply order is a mess

WhatsApp sorts by the latest message, so patient customers sink below people who spam. OrderLah queues by the first unanswered message.

Orders get written down wrong

Manual copying leads to wrong cakes and refunds. OrderLah reads the order, the customer confirms the summary, the owner approves.

Nobody follows up

Quiet customers are often comparing prices. OrderLah drafts a follow-up inside WhatsApp's 24-hour window — max 2, only when the owner taps send.
